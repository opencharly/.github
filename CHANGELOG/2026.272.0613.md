## Summary

**Byte-identical propagation of the native TypeScript coordination plugins from
`opencharly/opencharly` to the org dotgithub repo — including the round-2 review fixes
and the API-efficiency optimization, all in ONE commit.**

`.opencode/plugins/coord.ts` and `pr-watch.ts` are **PURE TypeScript**: they build the

- **`scripts/check-opencode-coord.mjs`** — the static + unit + B3 shell-vs-TS + real-HTTP
  efficiency/rate-limit gate (byte-identical).

- **`.opencode/instructions.md`** — reconciled to the native architecture (R5).

TypeScript; DO NOT MIX.** The maintainer-account R3 sign-off for this split is on
