# 2026.251.0844 — fix(pr-validator): move concurrency dedupe to the dispatchers (reusable must stay caller-compatible)

## Summary

Fixes the #163 dispatch-expansion regression at the root (RCA recorded): #84 put `cancel-in-progress` concurrency on the REUSABLE pr-validator workflow; callers whose installed dispatchers lack `actions: write` (the org default until now) fail to expand the reusable (0 jobs, instant failure — measured on opencharly #163 and every downstream PR). Diff accounting (2 files, resolved merge of main into feat/validator-legacy-fix at 78c8f4a):

1. `.github/workflows/pr-validator.yml` (the REUSABLE): the concurrency block is REMOVED — the reusable must stay caller-compatible (no `actions: write` requirement on legacy callers). A comment documents why.
2. `org-wide-pr-validator-dispatcher.yml` (the install template): the per-PR `concurrency` dedupe REMAINS with `permissions: actions: write` present (required for `cancel-in-progress`), plus a NOTE documenting the requirement; this is where the dedupe lives.

The change preserves the #38 dedupe at the dispatcher level, removes no validation, and adds no capability to the reusable.
